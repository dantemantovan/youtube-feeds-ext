Shorts blocker

When on, Shorts shelves, tiles, and the Shorts tab are hidden on youtube.com. Visiting /shorts/VIDEO redirects to the normal watch page.

Feed channels

Add a channel URL, @handle, or channel ID (UC…). IDs are resolved once and cached. Use the On/Off toggle to exclude a channel from feeds without deleting it. RSS returns ~15 latest videos per channel; Shorts links are dropped.

    Playlists

    Playlists are stored locally in this browser. Toggle On/Off to hide a playlist from the Feeds tab without deleting it. "Sync from YouTube" reads youtube.com/feed/playlists using your existing YouTube login (best effort). "Re-read file" picks up playlists.local.json placed in the extension folder.
